# Privacy policy — photos.jpeg

*Draft. It describes what the app does as of version 2.0.0; check it against the version you publish, add a contact address, and host it at a public web address.*

**photos.jpeg does not collect, send or share personal data.** It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

**Your photos and videos** are read from where you keep them and are never uploaded. Edits, ratings, keywords, the names you give to people and the catalogue itself are stored in a folder on your computer (`%APPDATA%\photos.jpeg`, or the app's private storage for the Store version).

**Faces.** If you use People → Find faces, the app looks for faces in your photos and stores a numeric description of each face, together with the names you type, in that same folder on your computer. This information is used only to group and find your own photos. It is not sent anywhere, and it is removed when you remove the person, the photo, or the catalogue folder.

**Automatic masks and keyword suggestions** are worked out by models that run on your computer. Nothing about your photos is sent to a service.

**Network use.** The app connects to the internet only in these cases:
- a build without bundled models asks before downloading a model file from the app's public repository on GitHub (the request carries nothing about you or your photos beyond what any download does, such as your IP address);
- map tiles, if you open the Map view, are fetched from the map provider for the area shown;
- updates, if an update address has been configured (not in the Store version, which the Store updates).

**Files the app writes.** Exports go where you choose. With the option turned on, edits are also written as XMP sidecar files next to your photos.

**Children.** The app is a general-purpose photo editor and is not directed at children.
