# Description

**Short description (up to 200 characters)**

A RAW photo and video editor that keeps your library on your own computer: develop, mask, retouch, merge and export, with no account and no upload.

**Description**

photos.jpeg is a photo editor in the manner of a darkroom catalogue: import folders of photos, sort and rate them, develop them without ever changing the original files, and export the results.

Everything runs on your computer. There is no account, no subscription and no cloud: the catalogue, the previews and the edits stay in a folder on your PC, and the photos stay where you keep them.

Develop
- RAW files from Nikon, Canon, Sony, Fujifilm, Olympus, Panasonic and DNG, developed from the sensor data and matched to the camera's own rendering
- Exposure, contrast, highlights and shadows, curves, colour mixer, colour grading, calibration, black and white
- Noise reduction and sharpening; lens profiles (distortion, vignetting, colour fringing) and a fisheye correction
- Crop, straighten, perspective and automatic upright
- Wide colour: RAW and phone photos can be edited and exported in Display P3, with a soft proof for sRGB and Adobe RGB

Masks and retouching
- Brush, linear and radial masks, with colour and brightness ranges, and masks combined with each other
- Subject, Sky, People, Background and single-person masks found automatically, on your computer
- Heal, clone, and content-aware fill for removing things from a photo

Merging
- HDR from bracketed exposures, hand-held, as a finished picture or as a DNG whose full range stays editable
- Panoramas from overlapping photos

Library
- Ratings, flags, colour labels, keywords, collections and smart collections
- Faces grouped by person and named by you; search by name
- Duplicate finder, map, compare view, stacks, virtual copies
- Reads and writes Lightroom / Camera Raw XMP sidecars

Export
- JPEG, PNG, WebP, TIFF (8 and 16 bit), AVIF and JPEG XL, with resizing, output sharpening, watermark and colour profile
- A background queue, so you keep working while files are written

Video
- Colour grading for clips with the same controls, camera log profiles, LUTs, stabilisation, and export to H.264, HEVC and ProRes
